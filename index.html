Two-step Gmail installation

Copy once. Paste into Gmail.

Fill in your details, choose a color and click Copy signature. The logo and contact icons are included automatically — no separate downloads or image uploads.

  1. 1

    Fill in your details

    Add your name, role and phone number.

  2. 2

    Choose your style

    Select a flavor theme and use Invert if you prefer the opposite color pairing.

  3. 3

    Copy signature

    Press the Copy signature button above. The entire signature is copied, including all images and links.

  4. 4

    Paste into Gmail

    Open Gmail → Settings → See all settings → Signature. Paste and save changes.
